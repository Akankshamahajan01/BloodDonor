THE MOTIVATION

Why We Engineered BloodConnect

Every two seconds, someone in India needs blood. Traditional coordination relies on chaotic phone calls and panic broadcasts. We built a structured algorithmic bridge.

The Traditional Challenge

The Flaws of Manual Coordination

In critical emergencies, hospital patients and their families are often left scrambling for donors across social media groups and private contacts.

  • ✕
    Delayed Response: Critical hours lost calling inactive or ineligible contacts.
  • ✕
    Compatibility Mistakes: High risk of requesting incompatible blood groups during panic.
  • ✕
    Donor Fatigue & Repeat Pings: Contacting donors who gave blood recently and are in a 90-day cooldown.
The BloodConnect Solution

Smart Digital Matching

BloodConnect provides a structured, responsive web application that streamlines registration, checks medical eligibility, and matches compatible donors instantly.

  • ✓
    Algorithmic Cross-Matching: Automated ABO & Rh factor matrix check before ranking.
  • ✓
    Built-in 90-Day Safety Clock: Calculates next eligible date to safeguard donor wellness.
  • ✓
    Direct Emergency Action: Click-to-call and WhatsApp integration for instant communication.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding blood donation eligibility, matching logic, and how to use BloodConnect.

How does the BloodConnect matching algorithm work? ▼
BloodConnect uses a deterministic rule-based biological cross-match matrix based on the ABO and Rh factor transfusion protocols. When a request is created for a particular blood group (e.g., A+), the system filters the donor registry to locate all medically compatible blood types (e.g., A+, A-, O+, O-), verifies that the donor is currently eligible (past their 90-day cooldown), and matches by geographic city location.
Who is eligible to donate blood? ▼
Generally, individuals aged 18 to 65 years, weighing at least 50 kg (110 lbs), with normal hemoglobin levels (≥ 12.5 g/dL) and good general health are eligible. Individuals must not have had tattoos or major surgery in the last 6 months and must adhere to the mandatory 90-day interval between donations.
Why is there a 90-day cooldown period between donations? ▼
After a whole blood donation, the human body replenishes fluid plasma within 24–48 hours, but red blood cells and iron reserves take approximately 8 to 12 weeks to fully regenerate. The 90-day (3-month) safety cooldown protects donors from anemia and fatigue while ensuring high-quality blood for recipients.
Is BloodConnect completely free to use? ▼
Yes! BloodConnect is 100% free and built as an academic social impact project for Basic Electrical & Electronics / Computing (BEE). There are no fees, subscriptions, or commercial transactions involved.
How is my donor information stored and protected? ▼
BloodConnect stores donor and request records securely in local client state (`localStorage`) without sending your private personal data to unverified third-party ad networks or brokers. You can manage your donor profile anytime from your personalized dashboard.
